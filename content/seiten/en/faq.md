# FAQ

Translation for page `faq`. Title: FAQ

The questions live in the template and are translated in Translate & Adapt under Theme → page.faq.

## Content

The most common questions about delivery, payment and returns.

## Suggested English wording for the template rows

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Wie lange dauert die Lieferung? | How long does delivery take? |
| Was kostet der Versand? | How much is shipping? |
| Kann meine Bestellung in mehreren Paketen ankommen? | Can my order arrive in more than one parcel? |
| Wie kann ich bezahlen? | How can I pay? |
| Wie funktioniert die Rückgabe? | How do returns work? |
| Ist das Leuchtmittel enthalten? | Is the light source included? |
| Wo finde ich Energielabel und Produktdatenblatt? | Where can I find the energy label and product information sheet? |
| Was tun, wenn die Leuchte beschädigt ankommt? | What if my lamp arrives damaged? |
| Wie erreiche ich den Support? | How do I reach support? |

Answers follow the German originals; open items stay marked `[[… — to be decided]]`.
